## Interpretation

The indicators overlap. 563 and 590 describe bindings and copy notes and must not automatically be counted as provenance. Former-owner access points provide identities, not necessarily places, dates or a chronological sequence. An absence of 561/361/fmo is not proof of absent provenance: other notes may contain evidence. Authority identifiers do not establish where a copy was owned. Multiple shelfmarks and field scope must be resolved before making copy-level claims. Counts are limited to exposed records from Z06 and are not a census of all ZB collections.

The 26 initial date warnings were inspected against publication statements. Later reproductions and the collection-level description were excluded. Corrected dates and ranges overlapping 1501–1525 remain explicit candidates rather than being silently discarded. The additional 23 hits from the broad range query are retained separately in range-only.xml and are not added to this year-query cohort.
